# Health risks the TruDoc Gold test menu can flag

Source: `TruDoc_Gold_Test_Menu_v4.xlsx` (core panel + annual tests with Include = Y). Each line is a distinct clinical risk or abnormality that a result outside its reference range can flag for a doctor to review. These are **flags, not diagnoses**: many need a repeat test or a reflex test to confirm (see the menu's Reflex Rules sheet).

**Needs TruDoc medical director sign-off before the number is used publicly.**

## Heart
1. High LDL cholesterol (hypercholesterolaemia): LDL, non-HDL, total cholesterol
2. Possible familial hypercholesterolaemia: LDL ≥190 mg/dL
3. High Lipoprotein(a), inherited heart risk: Lp(a)
4. High triglycerides (hypertriglyceridaemia): triglycerides, VLDL
5. Very high triglycerides (pancreatitis risk): triglycerides ≥500 mg/dL
6. Low HDL cholesterol: HDL, total cholesterol/HDL ratio
7. High residual risk from remnant cholesterol: remnant cholesterol
8. Raised 10-year heart attack and stroke risk: PREVENT score

## Diabetes and metabolism
9. Prediabetes: HbA1c, fasting glucose
10. Diabetes-range result (type and diagnosis need confirming): HbA1c, fasting glucose, eAG
11. Insulin resistance: TyG index, triglyceride/HDL ratio
12. Low blood sugar (hypoglycaemia): fasting glucose

## Blood
13. Iron-deficiency anaemia: haemoglobin, ferritin, TSAT, MCV
14. Iron deficiency without anaemia: ferritin, TSAT, serum iron
15. Microcytic anaemia / possible thalassaemia trait: MCV, MCH, RBC count
16. Macrocytic anaemia: MCV, haemoglobin
17. Anaemia of chronic disease pattern: haemoglobin, ferritin, TIBC
18. High red cell mass (polycythaemia): haemoglobin, haematocrit, RBC count
19. Possible iron overload (haemochromatosis): ferritin, TSAT
20. Low platelets (thrombocytopenia): platelet count
21. High platelets (thrombocytosis): platelet count
22. Abnormal red cell size variation (anisocytosis): RDW-CV, RDW-SD
23. Abnormal platelet size: MPV, PDW, plateletcrit
24. Immature red cells in the blood (possible bone marrow stress): NRBC
25. Possible red cell breakdown (haemolysis): indirect bilirubin, haemoglobin, urobilinogen

## Immunity and inflammation
26. Low white cell count (leukopenia): WBC
27. High white cell count (leukocytosis): WBC
28. Low neutrophils (neutropenia): neutrophils
29. High neutrophils, possible bacterial infection (neutrophilia): neutrophils
30. Low lymphocytes (lymphopenia): lymphocytes
31. High lymphocytes, possible viral infection (lymphocytosis): lymphocytes
32. High monocytes (monocytosis): monocytes
33. High eosinophils, allergy or parasites (eosinophilia): eosinophils
34. High basophils (basophilia): basophils
35. Immature white cells in the blood (left shift): immature granulocytes
36. Acute inflammation or infection: CRP >10 mg/L
37. Raised inflammation ratios: NLR, PLR

## Liver
38. Fatty liver disease (MASLD) risk: ALT, AST, GGT, triglycerides
39. Advanced liver fibrosis risk: FIB-4
40. Liver cell injury: ALT, AST
41. Alcohol-related liver injury pattern: GGT, AST/ALT ratio
42. Blocked bile flow (cholestasis): ALP, GGT, direct bilirubin
43. Possible Gilbert syndrome: indirect bilirubin
44. High bilirubin (hyperbilirubinaemia): total bilirubin
45. Low albumin, poor nutrition or liver function (hypoalbuminaemia): albumin, total protein
46. High globulins (chronic inflammation or immune activity): globulin, A/G ratio
47. Low total protein: total protein

## Kidney
48. Chronic kidney disease (reduced filtration): eGFR, creatinine
49. Dehydration pattern: BUN/creatinine ratio, urine specific gravity
50. Sudden kidney function drop (acute kidney injury flag): creatinine, BUN
51. High uric acid (hyperuricaemia) / gout risk: uric acid

## Electrolytes and acid-base
52. Low sodium (hyponatraemia): sodium
53. High sodium (hypernatraemia): sodium
54. Low potassium (hypokalaemia): potassium
55. High potassium (hyperkalaemia): potassium
56. Low chloride (hypochloraemia): chloride
57. High chloride (hyperchloraemia): chloride
58. Metabolic acidosis: bicarbonate, anion gap
59. Metabolic alkalosis: bicarbonate

## Bone and minerals
60. High calcium (hypercalcaemia): calcium, corrected calcium
61. Low calcium (hypocalcaemia): calcium, corrected calcium
62. Possible bone turnover disorder: ALP with normal GGT

## Thyroid
63. Hypothyroidism (underactive thyroid): TSH, Free T4
64. Subclinical hypothyroidism: TSH, Free T4
65. Hyperthyroidism (overactive thyroid): TSH, Free T4
66. Subclinical hyperthyroidism: TSH, Free T4


## Prostate (men 50+)
67. Raised PSA, prostate cancer screening flag: PSA

## Vitamins
68. Vitamin D deficiency: vitamin D
69. Vitamin D insufficiency: vitamin D
70. Vitamin B12 deficiency: vitamin B12

## Urinary
71. Urinary tract infection: nitrite, leukocyte esterase, urine WBC, bacteria
72. Blood in urine (haematuria): occult blood, urine RBC
73. Protein in urine (proteinuria): urine protein
74. Sugar in urine (glycosuria): urine glucose
75. Ketones in urine (ketonuria): urine ketones
76. Kidney stone risk: urine crystals, urine pH, uric acid
77. Bilirubin in urine (bilirubinuria): urine bilirubin
78. Abnormal urobilinogen: urobilinogen
79. Kidney-origin urine sediment: urine casts
80. Fungal urinary infection: yeast

## Biological age
81. Faster biological ageing: biological age (PhenoAge), age gap (needs CRP reported to ≤0.3 mg/L; open item with the lab)

**Total: 81 distinct health risks.**
